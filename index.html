    About

    I have always dreamed of telling meaningful stories. As a child, I imagined fairies and woodland spirits watching over me, and that imagination soon turned into writing novels at nine.

    Later, I ran short Dungeons & Dragons sessions, whether spooky, mysterious or just plain silly, and discovered how much I love guiding players through the chaos I craft. That led me to narrative-driven games, from playful, pun-filled tales to deeper branching stories.

    Designing platformer levels, to designing maps, that though, was a blast. Of course, what good is a level if we can't paired it with great mechanics. Meaning and intention soon became my focus.
